|---|---|---|

| 8 | Team productivity (velocity, cycle time, on-time, blocked) | tab "Team productivity" |
| 9 | Payroll cost and arithmetic reconciliation | tab "Payroll" |
| 10 | Budget vs actual and KPI targets | tab "Budget vs actual" |
